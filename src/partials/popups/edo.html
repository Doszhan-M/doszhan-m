Uchet.EDO

Uchet.EDO is a document exchange service for Kazakhstan. Companies sign acts, invoices and contracts with a digital signature and send them to counterparties, and those documents hold the same legal weight as paper. More than 200,000 companies use it, from small businesses to Wolt, Glovo and 2GIS.

Here I worked as team lead and tech lead rather than a developer. I led a small team, owned the technical direction and the decisions behind it, and spent most of my time on two things: keeping the service reliable at that scale, and helping the people on the team grow.
